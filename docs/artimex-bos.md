# Artimex — Website Content & Validation Brief

**Status:** Draft for client review
**Primary website language:** English
**Localized route:** `/es/` for Spanish content
**Purpose:** Confirm the site structure, content priorities, and factual claims before publication.

## 1. Purpose of this document

This is a content and validation brief for the Artimex website. It is not public-facing website copy and it does not define the visual design. It gives the client a clear list of proposed pages, content blocks, and facts that must be approved before publication.

## 2. Proposed site structure

| Route | Purpose | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Brand introduction, fresh offer, and first conversion | Shop fresh |
| `/es/` | Spanish-language version of the core experience | Comprar fresco |
| `/about/` | Brand story, craft, and credibility | Talk to sales |
| `/products/` | Approved product catalogue | Request catalogue |
| `/wholesale/` | Retail, restaurant, and foodservice offer | Request wholesale access |
| `/news/` | Press and company updates | Read article |
| `/contact/` | Sales routing and location details | Contact sales |

These routes are proposed and remain subject to the client's commercial priorities and available content.

## 3. Homepage content direction

1. **Hero** — Present Artimex as an authentic Mexican bakery and clearly distinguish fresh and frozen offerings.
2. **Brand story** — Show the craft, people, and tradition behind the products.
3. **Product discovery** — Lead with a curated selection, then provide access to the full wholesale catalogue.
4. **Business value** — Explain consistency, labor savings, waste reduction, and operational simplicity for business customers.
5. **Preparation and service** — Show the practical path from storage to preparation, baking, and sale.
6. **Proof** — Use only approved metrics, customer references, certifications, press, and production capabilities.
7. **Contact** — Give visitors a clear sales contact and qualify wholesale enquiries.

## 4. Recommended content records

| Content record | Required information |
| --- | --- |
| Brand profile | Name, signature, story, audiences, approved proof points |
| Product | Name, category, description, image, format, packaging, storage, preparation, allergens, availability |
| Channel | Fresh retail, wholesale, foodservice, or distribution; CTA and qualification fields |
| Story block | Page, order, eyebrow, title, copy, image, and locale |
| Proof point | Metric or reference, source, date, and approval status |
| Press item | Title, publication, date, URL, excerpt, and image |
| Contact point | Email, phone, address, and routing label |

Each record should have a clear status, source, owner, last-reviewed date, and locale.

## 5. Editorial workflow

**Draft → Internal review → Client validation → Published → Periodic review**

Any factual claim without a reliable source or client approval must remain out of public proof sections until it is validated.

## 6. Decisions required from the client

- Is the main commercial priority fresh retail, wholesale, foodservice, or a shared journey?
- Which products are currently available, in which formats, and in which territories?
- Which production metrics are current and approved for publication?
- Which customer references, certifications, and press links may be shown publicly?
- What is the lead-to-order process after `sales@artimex.com`?
- Which product catalogue, formats, packaging, storage instructions, and allergen information can be provided?

## 7. Claims requiring confirmation
