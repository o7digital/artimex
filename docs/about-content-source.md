# About Artimex: content and provenance

Source: [Artimex's original About Us page](https://www.artimexbakery.com/aboutus.html), reviewed on 28 September 2026. The new English and Spanish copy lives in `src/data/about.js` and is rendered by `src/components/AboutSection.astro`.

The section brings together the founding purpose, historical El Gallo Giro connection, artisan baking details, everyday bread occasions and the role of flash-frozen bakery technology. Original Artimex bread, concha and empanada photos are retained. The former archive block is integrated into the craft story rather than repeating a separate generic introduction and assortment photo.

El Gallo Giro is described as part of Artimex's origins. The copy does not assert a current supply contract, endorsement, restaurant count or volume. The legacy page's undated figures (21 years, 11 restaurants and annual meal counts) are omitted. No founding date, certification, shelf life, ingredient specification or new production metric has been added. Operational benefits are described without a savings or sales guarantee.

Fresh and wholesale panels use the existing product visibility settings, including their section links. Disabling a product section also removes its corresponding About panel. The company history and craft content remain available.

Delivery stays on `dev` with a Vercel Preview deployment, per the user's instruction.
