# Artimex product collection

Source checked on September 28, 2026: [Artimex — Our Breads](https://www.artimexbakery.com/Our_Breads.html), which displays the [original product catalogue](https://www.artimexbakery.com/image/cat_List.jpg).

The Fresh collection includes all 27 named product varieties from that sheet. Concha sizes are grouped under their four colors. Each of the three named polvorones has its own record; the source provides one shared assortment photograph, identified as such on those cards. Bolillos and teleras are retained from the previous collection and are not additions from this sheet.

CSS clips each photograph from this unchanged, locally served asset. No external scripts or tracking are imported.

English and Spanish product descriptions are based on the source names, family descriptions and photographs. Guayabas are described as sweet scones, without assuming a guava filling. Cuernos de Fino use a general sweet-paste description because the English source calls the paste yellow while the photograph shows pink.

The sheet is titled “Frozen — Ready to Bake.” At the user's request its names and photographs are presented in the Fresh collection, but frozen case counts, weights, pallet formats and preparation instructions are not attached to these Fresh cards. Fresh availability, prices, ingredients, allergens and retail pack sizes remain to be confirmed with Artimex. The collection directs visitors to the bakery for availability.
