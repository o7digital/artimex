## Business/legal review before treating this as a company-wide compliance program

- Confirm the legal entity operating the site and any separate notices for purchases, employees, applicants or offline records.
- Determine whether Artimex is covered by the CCPA. The current CPPA threshold is gross annual revenue of at least $26.625 million for the preceding calendar year, or the applicable data-volume/revenue tests. Do not use the old unadjusted $25 million threshold.
- If covered, provide the required request methods, including a toll-free number when required for a business that does not operate exclusively online. The supplied 562 number is not a toll-free number. Website email links do not solve a missing toll-free-number requirement.
- Validate the company-wide data categories, sources, recipients and sale/sharing activity for the legally required lookback period. The published notice makes only current-website sale/sharing statements.
- Confirm retention criteria, email/hosting provider arrangements and safeguards. No unsupported fixed retention duration is published.
- Assign responsibility for privacy requests arriving at sales@artimex.com, postal requests, verification, authorized agents and appeals. Meet applicable CCPA acknowledgment/response/opt-out periods where covered.
- Review applicant-notice obligations at the point of collection if a separate recruitment workflow is introduced.
